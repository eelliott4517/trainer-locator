# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Seven trainers were pinned 100 to 325 yards from where they stand: Ander Germaine, Ilsa Corbin,
  Osborne the Night Man, Jennea Cannon, Thorfin Stoneshield and Brother Benjamin in Stormwind, and
  Alma Jainrose in Redridge. They're in the right place now.
- 38 more trainers (595 in all). 30 from the original game whose Wowhead pages don't say what they teach: Malosh
  and Haromm in Stonard, Captain Evencane in Theramore, Jannos Lighthoof at Camp Mojache, Nittlebur
  Sparkfizzle in Ironforge, Anishar in Darnassus, the pet trainers Grokor and Zudd, and 22 profession
  trainers, most of them the specialization trainers (armor and weapon crafters, gnome and goblin
  engineers, dragonscale, elemental and tribal leatherworkers, shadoweave tailors). And six of
  Forever's own that Wowhead doesn't count as trainers: the shaman and warrior trainers at Aerie Peak,
  the artisan weapon crafter and armorsmith on Zephras Isle, and the weapons trainer and expert
  enchanter in Riverglades. And two that are new on Wowhead: Kaylaena Springwhisper (herbalism) and
  Ortak Lomgok (cooking) on Mount Hyjal.
- Eight trainers whose factions Wowhead hasn't recorded were hidden from everyone, among them Grungle
  and Melbin Powderfuse in Riverglades and Master Mycologist Shurome in Felwood. Both factions see
  them now.
- Opening the map in combat no longer causes an error from the trainer pins. A right-click on a pin
  backs the map out, as anywhere else on the map. In combat, clicking a trainer in the list puts the
  map pin on them but leaves the map shut.
- Shift-clicking a trainer to link the spot in chat no longer moves your own map pin.
- Trainer data refreshed from Wowhead.

## 1.1.0

- A minimap button: click it for the trainer list, right-click for the options, and drag it to
  move it around the minimap's edge (it remembers where). Hide or show it from the options page or
  with `/trainers minimap`.

## 1.0.0

- A window listing every trainer in WoW: Forever by kind: each class, weapon masters, the nine
  professions, cooking, first aid and fishing, riding, pet and portal trainers. Nearest first,
  grouped by continent, with distance and coordinates.
- Click a trainer for the game's map pin and its arrow in the world (and a TomTom waypoint when
  TomTom is installed). Shift-click links the spot in chat. Right-click clears the pin.
- Pins on the world map for the kind picked, with the same tooltip.
- Greys out trainers who can't help you: a starting-area class trainer whose spells stop below your
  level, a profession trainer who can't take you past your rank, the other faction's.
- A search box, `/trainers <kind or search>`, the minimap's addon menu, a key binding and a page in
  the game's options.
